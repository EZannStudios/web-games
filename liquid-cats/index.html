About the game

Turn the square maze a quarter at a time and gravity follows, pouring squishy cat blobs through doors and into boxes they have to fill exactly.

Cats of the same breed merge into bigger cats, different breeds fight, and the maze doors reshuffle every 10 seconds. Play Classic, Cat Flood or Cat Flaps, with power-ups to save a run.

Features

  • Three modes: Classic, Cat Flood and Cat Flaps.
  • Same-breed cats merge into bigger cats, up to four in one blob.
  • Maze doors reshuffle every 10 seconds, so every turn counts.
  • Power-ups to rescue a run.
  • Music and recorded meows.

How to play

  1. Turn the maze with the round arrow buttons or by swiping across it. Gravity follows each turn.
  2. Pour a cat into a box it fills exactly to send it home and score.
  3. Cats of the same breed merge, up to 4 cats. In Classic and Cat Flaps, two different breeds touching ends the run; in Cat Flaps each cat leaves through the flap of its colour.
  4. In Cat Flood, don't let the maze fill up.
  5. Use power-ups when a run gets tight.

Keyboard: Left / Right arrows or A / D to turn the maze, 1 / 2 / 3 for power-ups.

From the developer

I started this as jelly cats in a spinning wheel, and it just didn't feel right, so I rebuilt it as a square maze you turn a quarter at a time. The cats are blobs of little squares that pour like sand, and getting them to squeeze through doors and still keep their faces on took a few tries. I capped merges at four cats because bigger ones couldn't fit anywhere. Which mode kept you playing longer, Cat Flood or Cat Flaps?
