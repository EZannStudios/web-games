About the game

Your ball auto-orbits a glowing core. Tap to flip your direction and weave through waves of shapes closing in from every side.

Skim past enemies for near-miss combo points, grab power-ups — a shield, slow-time, and a supernova that clears the screen — and see how long your nerves hold. One tap, infinite dodging.

Features

  • One-tap controls.
  • Near-miss combos for daring dodges.
  • Three power-ups: shield, slow-time and supernova.
  • Coins to earn and skins to unlock: Classic, Neon Streaks, Food Fight and Space.
  • Online leaderboard on Playgama.

How to play

  1. Your ball circles the central core automatically.
  2. Tap the screen to reverse your orbit direction instantly.
  3. Time your reversals to slip past the enemies flying in, and skim close for near-miss combo points.
  4. Collect pickups: a blue shield blocks one hit, a purple ring slows every enemy for a few seconds, and the yellow nova star wipes the screen.
  5. Survive as long as you can: your score climbs the longer you last.

Keyboard: Space to reverse.
