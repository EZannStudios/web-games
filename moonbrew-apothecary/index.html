About the game

Every night, sweetly spooky customers come to your window: a shivering scarecrow, a moth lady who loves your lamp, a lonely ghost who can't remember her name. Brew their potions in your bubbling cauldron and stir them just right for a 3-star finish.

When the pantry runs dry, step into the moonlit wood and cross the Veil to the Other Side, where glowing spirit flowers bloom. Earn coins, upgrade your gear, decorate your shop, and help the Lonely Ghost find her way home.

Features

  • Brew, stir and serve potions for a cast of sweetly spooky customers.
  • Gather ingredients in the moonlit wood, then cross the Veil for spirit flowers.
  • Upgrade your gear and decorate your shop.
  • Help the Lonely Ghost remember who she is and find her way home.
  • A cat familiar to pet, recipe books, and a crystal ball that glimpses the next order.
  • Cozy Halloween fun with a little tension and no jump scares.

How to play

  1. Brew: read the order ticket, then tap ingredients in the pantry to drop them in the cauldron.
  2. Stir: drag in circles around the cauldron. Keep the needle in the green for a 3-star potion.
  3. Serve: tap the customer to hand the potion over. Some customers want two potions.
  4. Gather: out of ingredients? Walk out the door, hold where you want to go, and walk over plants to pick them.
  5. Cross the Veil: tap the eye button. Spirit flowers only grow on the Other Side. Come back before your soul candle goes out, and keep away from the Lost Ones.
  6. At dawn, spend coins on upgrades and shop decorations.

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to walk, Space to cross the Veil, R / C for the books.
