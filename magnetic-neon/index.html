About the game

Your ball rolls forever through a neon outrun world. Hold anywhere to fire a magnet beam that pulls it toward your finger, curving it over cliffs, around walls and through deadly spike fields.

But the magnet is fuel: it drains while you hold it, and the only way to refuel is grabbing glowing gold orbs. Precision, timing and nerve — how far into the neon can you drift?

Features

  • One-touch magnet steering.
  • A fuel meter that makes every hold count.
  • Cliffs, magenta walls and neon spike fields.
  • An endless run: distance is your score.
  • Online leaderboard for the best distance on Playgama.

How to play

  1. Hold anywhere on the screen to switch on your magnet. The ball is pulled toward that point, so move your finger to steer it.
  2. Release to let momentum and gravity carry it.
  3. Arc over cliffs and gaps, lift over the magenta walls before you stall, and thread the neon spike fields.
  4. Grab gold orbs to refuel: the magnet drains while you hold it.
  5. Survive as long as you can. Distance travelled is your score.
