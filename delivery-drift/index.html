About the game

Drift a delivery van down a winding road as the day turns to night and back to dawn: desert dunes, a moonlit cemetery, a neon city. Dodge oil slicks, cow skulls, ghosts, oncoming cars and a road-wrecking UFO, because every hit knocks packages out of the back.

Earn coins with every run, upgrade your van in the garage, and find out what waits at the end of the road.

Features

  • One-touch drifting that is easy to learn and hard to master.
  • A road that runs from desert noon through a moonlit cemetery and a neon city to a new dawn.
  • Hazards that change with the time of day: oil slicks, cow skulls, ghosts, oncoming cars, a police cruiser and a road-wrecking UFO.
  • A garage with five upgrades: more cargo, off-road tires, brighter headlights, a cowcatcher and a faster nitro.
  • Something big waits at the end of the road.

How to play

  1. Hold the left or right half of the screen (touch or mouse) to drift that way, and let go to straighten up.
  2. Packages are your lives. Leaving the road, or hitting trees, oil, skulls, cars and other hazards, knocks packages out. When the van is empty, the run is over.
  3. When the nitro tank is full, tap it for a speed boost.
  4. The distance you drive turns into coins. Spend them in the Garage.

Keyboard: A / D or the arrow keys to drift, Space for nitro.

From the developer

It started as a game where drifting flung packages into roadside mailboxes for points. That never felt right, so the packages became your life bar instead, and the mailboxes turned into trees you really don't want to hit. The mountains behind the road took a few rounds too. The first ones looked so much like pyramids that I kept them as pyramids for a while before turning them into a proper range. How far did you get before your last package fell off?
