About

EzannStudios is an independent game studio focused on lightweight HTML5 arcade and casual games. We build tight, one-more-try experiences — easy to pick up, hard to put down — with clean visuals, responsive one-touch controls, and instant browser play across desktop and mobile. New titles are released regularly across web gaming portals.
